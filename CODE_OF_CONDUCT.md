# Code of Conduct

We want Duplex to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone who uses or contributes to it.
This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good intent.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents
the project in public. Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer by opening a private security advisory draft or, if
that is unavailable, a public issue titled "Conduct report" **without details** so a private channel
can be arranged. Reports are handled confidentially and with respect for the reporter.
